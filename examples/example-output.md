# Example: `/prep-words quarterly review`

After a session preparing slides for a quarterly review at a regional health
network. (Names and figures from the session are left out on purpose.)

## Words

| Word | Topic | Say it |
|---|---|---|
| provider | Operations | pro-VI-der |
| mobile clinic | Operations | MO-bile CLIN-ic |
| utilization | Operations | check |
| reimbursement | Funding | re-im-BURSE-ment |
| allocate | Funding | AL-lo-cate |
| increase (noun) | Funding | IN-crease |
| analytical | Data & reporting | an-a-LYT-i-cal |
| interoperability | Data & reporting | check |
| dashboard | Data & reporting | DASH-board |

## Sentences

### Operations
1. Our **providers** rotate through the **mobile clinic** twice a week.
2. **Utilization** went up after we added the evening **mobile clinic**.
3. Each **provider** now sees more patients without longer waits.

### Funding
1. We expect **reimbursement** to cover most of the new visits.
2. The board will **allocate** the **increase** to staffing first.
3. Faster **reimbursement** lets us **allocate** money where it matters.

### Data & reporting
1. Our **analytical** team built one **dashboard** for every site.
2. **Interoperability** between the two systems is still our biggest gap.
3. The **dashboard** shows **utilization** by site and by week.
